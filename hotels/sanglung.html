Days 5-6 • Tejakula

Sanglung Villas

Wild isolation on Bali's forgotten north coast. Where the mountains meet the sea, and tourists never come.

Why Here

The Wild Isolation

After the lush mountains of Munduk, we descend to Bali's driest, most dramatic coast. Tejakula is a world away from the tourist trail — this is the Bali that existed before the crowds.

Sanglung Villas sits perched between volcanic mountains and the Bali Sea. The landscape here is stark and beautiful: black lava rock, traditional salt farms, and fishermen who've worked these waters for generations.

The boutique property offers intimate luxury in complete seclusion. Private villas with plunge pools. Exceptional dining with ocean views. Snorkeling in protected coral reefs.

This is where you truly disconnect.
