Days 7-8 • Sebatu

Bambootel

Sustainable bamboo luxury in the quieter Ubud. Where eco-conscious design meets rice terrace views.

Why Here

The Sustainable Sanctuary

Ubud can be overwhelming — the traffic, the crowds, the commercialization. Sebatu is the answer: all of Ubud's spiritual energy, none of its chaos.

Bambootel represents a new vision of luxury. The stunning structures are built almost entirely from bamboo — one of the world's most sustainable materials. It's proof that eco-conscious doesn't mean compromising on comfort.

Wake up to views of rice terraces. Practice yoga in open-air pavilions. Float in the infinity pool that seems to merge with the jungle canopy.

From here, we'll explore Bali's most sacred temples — Tirta Empul, Gunung Kawi Sebatu, and the ancient Gunung Kawi — all before the crowds arrive.
