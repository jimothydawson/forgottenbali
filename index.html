The Expedition Path

Ten Days of
Discovery.

We move with the sun. From the black sand of the west to the misty ridges of the north, every stop is intentional. No filler. No tourist traps.

01

The Gathering & Decompression

Meet at WakaGangga (Tabanan). Sunset on black sand beaches. A slow reset after travel. We meet where the waves meet the volcano.

Base: WakaGangga

02-04

Into the Clouds

Ancient UNESCO terraces, the Bayan Tree, and the "Star of the Show" — Munduk Moding Plantation. Three nights of mountain magic, yoga, and coffee forest treks.

Base: Munduk Moding Plantation

05

The King of Waterfalls

We trek Sekumpul en route to the dry north. This is the raw Bali. Check in to the wild isolation of Sanglung Villas.

Base: Sanglung Villas

06

The Forgotten Coast

Yeh Mampeh Waterfall and the salt farmers of Tejakula. This is where the tourists end and the island begins.

Base: Sanglung Villas

07-08

The Crater Rim & Sacred Temples

Drive up the volcano. Lunch with a view at Kintamani. Tirta Empul at sunrise. We settle into the sustainable luxury of Bambootel.

Base: Bambootel (Sebatu)

09-10

The Soft Landing

A final transfer to Sanur. Happy hour, farewell dinner, and beachfront bliss at Maya Sanur. A luxury finish for a wild journey.

Base: Maya Sanur
