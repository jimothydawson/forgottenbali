The Expedition Path.

Night 1

The Gathering

We meet in Canggu at The Bohemian Bali. A single night purely for convenience and logistics. It is our staging ground—a final taste of high-design "Designer Bali" before we head into the wild. The Bohemian is an architectural masterpiece that sets our luxury standard.

Nights 2-3

UNESCO Immersion

A 90-minute escape to the deep green at Nadi Nature Resort, Baturiti. Luxury glamping on the edge of the UNESCO-listed Jatiluwih rice terraces. Total silence and immersion in ancient nature.

Nights 4-5

Above the Clouds

Transition to the cool northern highlands at Munduk Moding Plantation. Home to the iconic infinity pool overlooking the northern coast and working coffee plantations.

Nights 6-7

The Sacred Finale

We descend into the valley of the kings at Aksari Resort, Tampaksiring. Includes a private, silent sunrise purification at Pura Mengening—the "Still Temple" that tourists haven't found yet.
